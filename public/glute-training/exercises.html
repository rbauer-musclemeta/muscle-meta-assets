How to read the comfort filters. They narrow the list to exercises that are typically easier on a given area: kind to knees limits deep knee bend, spine-conscious avoids loading a bent spine, low balance demand needs no single-leg stability, and hip-comfort avoids deep hip bend and side-lying on the hip. They are a guide, not a prescription. Pain is your signal to change the exercise.
