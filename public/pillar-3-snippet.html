The Catabolic Cascade: Why Recovery Is Non-Negotiable

Chronic cortisol elevation activates the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway and autophagy-lysosome pathway in skeletal muscle — the same mitochondrial degradation machinery exercise stimulates, but without the subsequent anabolic rebound. Sleep deprivation (<6h) elevates IL-6, TNF-α, and NF-κB, directly antagonizing PGC-1α transcription. The result: mitochondrial fragmentation, reduced oxidative capacity, and accelerated muscle protein breakdown. Restoring parasympathetic tone via HRV-guided recovery is the single highest-leverage intervention for maintaining the anabolic window created by Pillars 1 and 2.

Evidence basis: Dattilo et al. (2011), Cote et al. (2022) — HPA axis & skeletal muscle catabolism; Spiegel et al. (1999) — sleep deprivation GH suppression
