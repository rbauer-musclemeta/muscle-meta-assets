A practical safe start

Your body, your pace

Educational ideas to talk through with your care team, not a prescription.

1

Strength first

Build comfortable, controlled strength on a stable movement such as a chair stand or supported step-up.

2

Add speed with control

When ready, move up with intent and come down slowly. Fast does not mean rushed.

3

Stop when form fades

End a set when speed or form drops. Quality beats quantity.

4

Add in small steps

After illness, surgery or time off your feet, start from where you are today and add load and speed gradually, with your care team's guidance.

5

Track what you notice

Standing smoothly from your usual chair is a good conversation starter with a clinician.
