## Kept qualitative or left out

- "About 2% per year after 40": not displayed (verdict: does not hold as stated).
- "Power falls 2 to 3x faster than strength", "Type II loss 2x faster", "RFD 3 to 5% a year": left out (RESEARCH-NEEDED); qualitative wording used.
- Fast-twitch fibers are shown as smaller, never as lost or disappearing.
- Disuse hitting Type II fibers or power first or most: not claimed.
- "18-month decline spiral", "bone demineralization precedes muscle atrophy": not used.
- Power training and falls or independence: explicitly not claimed; on-screen note says falls were not tested.
- Curves and fiber diagram are labeled illustrative or schematic and carry no data points.
